# frameworks/

The repeatable systems behind the styling advice. These are the things that turn
opinions into something I can apply consistently across clients and posts.

## What belongs here

- **Colour system** — how colours are grouped, named and matched to a person;
  the rules for combining them and the vocabulary I use to describe them.
- **Coverage matrix** — the grid of wardrobe needs (occasion, season, formality,
  layer) used to spot gaps and over-buying.
- **Material rubric** — how fabrics and construction are scored: fibre content,
  weight, drape, durability, care, price-per-wear expectations.

## What doesn't belong here

Brand-specific judgements (`brands/`) and anything written for publication
(`content/`). A framework should outlive any single brand or post.

## Suggested files

- `colour-system.md`
- `coverage-matrix.md`
- `material-rubric.md`
