# Palette outfits, 24 Sept: Note angles

**Not my voice yet.** `voice/` holds its README and no writing samples, so
nothing here can be matched to how the owner writes.

**These are not finished Notes.** CLAUDE.md hard rule 1: *never write final
published text; if asked to "write the note", produce three angles and a rough
shape. The owner writes the words.* Each Note below therefore has three angles
(what the first line does, in which register) and one rough shape (the
line-image-nothing skeleton from `content/boards/style-spec.md` A1). The
bracketed parts are for the owner to fill.

The rules every version keeps (from the brief of 24 Sept and style-spec A1):
one or two lines, the first line carries it, the images do the work, no
closing line, no sign-off, no question at the end unless the question *is* the
first line. Taste statements yes ("I styled", "I loved"); nothing that happened
to the owner; nothing about her own colouring or season; no prices; no engine
terms; products shown, not labelled.

## 1. One Note, all three boards

**Idea.** One brown carries all three: chocolate, mahogany and cinnamon, then a
dark near-black brown under teal. Each board puts a different colour on it:
pink and red, rust tones on rust, teal and aubergine. The season's combination
is *a deep brown base plus one colour you would not expect on it*.

**Attach, in this order (three-up row):**
1. `content/outfits/2026-09-24-pink-on-chocolate.jpg`
2. `content/outfits/2026-09-24-tonal-brown.jpg`
3. `content/outfits/2026-09-24-teal-on-earth.jpg`

**Angles**
- **Label.** A bare lowercase noun phrase naming the combination: the brown
  plus the three colours laid on it. No verb, no full stop. [two to six words]
- **Verdict.** A flat first-person taste statement about the brown doing the
  work (which colour it makes look best is the owner's call). Sentence case,
  full stop. [one sentence]
- **Question.** Ask which of the three the reader would wear first, as the
  first line. The replies are the rest. [one short question]

**Rough shape**
```
[first line: one of the three angles]
[board 1] [board 2] [board 3]
```
No second line. If one is wanted, it names the three pairings as three short
fragments, no brands.
