# data/

Reference data and measurement — the numbers side of the project.

## What belongs here

- **UTM scheme** — the naming convention for campaign links: source, medium,
  campaign and content values, and rules for how they're constructed so links
  stay consistent and reports stay clean.
- **Metrics** — subscriber counts, open and click rates, post performance,
  consultation funnel numbers. Whatever is worth tracking over time.

## What doesn't belong here

Interpretation of the numbers — that's a note or a post. Keep this folder to the
scheme and the raw figures.

## Suggested files

- `utm-scheme.md`
- `metrics.csv` (or one file per reporting period)
