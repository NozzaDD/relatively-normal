# consultations/

One folder per beta consultation, named `YYYY-MM-DD-client-name/` (first name or
an initial is fine — these are real people).

## What belongs in each folder

- Intake notes and what they asked for
- Photos, measurements and wardrobe inventory
- The recommendations delivered, and the reasoning behind them
- Follow-up notes: what worked, what didn't, what I'd do differently

## Why it matters

Consultations are where the frameworks get tested. Patterns that show up across
several folders should be promoted into `frameworks/` or `content/note-bank.md`.

## Privacy

This is personal client information. Keep it out of published content unless the
client has agreed, and anonymise anything that ends up in a post.
